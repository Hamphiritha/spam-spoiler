How the pipeline works

1

Collect & preprocess

Incoming mail is normalized — stop words removed, stemmed, tokenized and padded.

2

Extract features

Word-cloud frequency counts and TF-IDF weighting turn each message into a feature vector.

3

Classify

An LSTM/GRU network scores the message across four classes for forensic review.

4

Decide

Results land in the inbox view, flagged and ready for evidence or action.
